GazeVeil

Passive website checks for India's DPDP Act.

What we do

We look only at what any visitor can already see on a public website, such as cookie banners, privacy notices, consent wording and how forms collect data. We send you a short PDF report in plain language.

What you get

  • A free sample report.
  • A detailed report with fix steps, which we only do with the site owner's written permission.

What we do not do

  • We never log in, and we never guess passwords.
  • We never touch your systems or probe for weaknesses unless you have given us written permission for a deeper check.
  • We do not say a website is "compliant".
  • This is not legal advice.

You can read exactly what we look at, and how we rate what we see, on the How we check page.
